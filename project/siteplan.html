Site Plan: CrumbPop

CrumbPop

Name: CrumbPop

Description: I chose the name 'CrumbPop' because of the wordplay—it just felt right for the brand. I really wanted the name to reflect how cheerful and fun the desserts are, with the ultimate goal of brightening up the day for every single customer.

Site Purpose

I’m building this site because I’m really passionate about baking and I want to help small, local bakeries grow. The site acts as a hub where these shops can showcase their menu, recieve reviews, and connect with their community. My goal is to make it easier for people to discover great local desserts while giving small business owners a platform that actually looks as good as their products taste.

User Scenarios

These are the questions our customers are usually looking to answer when they visit the site:

  • What kind of cakes and desserts are available on the menu today?
  • How can I place a custom order for my upcoming event or special celebration?
  • Are there any seasonal products available right now?

Color Scheme

  • Pink (#d6335a): Used for call-to-action buttons and primary headers to create a welcoming, energetic feel.
  • Yellow (#d9b300): Used for special offer badges, highlights, and decorative elements to add warmth and cheer.

Typography

Headings Font: 'Unbounded', sans-serif (via Google Fonts).

Body Font: 'Poppins', sans-serif (via Google Fonts).

Application: I'm using a mix of these two fonts to balance style and readability. I chose 'Unbounded' for the headings because it's fun and bold—it matches the cheerful personality of the brand and our bright color scheme. For the body text, I went with 'Poppins' because it’s super clean and makes the page really easy to read for the customers.
